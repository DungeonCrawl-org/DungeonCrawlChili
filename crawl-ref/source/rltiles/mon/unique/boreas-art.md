## Generation prompt

Create ONE ORIGINAL pixel-art monster sprite for Dungeon Crawl Chili: BOREAS, THE NORTH WIND, a Greek myth inspired male winter wind spirit. Asset: a single 32 by 32 logical pixel roguelike sprite, enlarged using nearest-neighbor pixel blocks for the delivered image. Design for strong readability when reduced to 32x32 actual pixels. Subject: an imposing elderly male with a clearly visible long white wind-swept beard, icy pale-blue skin, fierce cyan eyes, a blue-grey Greek mantle flowing into two curling gusts beneath his body, and two broad swept-back feathered wings tipped with frost. He floats, faces slightly toward the viewer's right in a 3/4 game-sprite stance, and spreads his empty hands as if summoning the north wind. White hair and beard must be a defining part of his silhouette. Restrained 12–18 color palette: ice white, pale cyan, slate blue, deep navy, dark charcoal outlining. Chunky hand-placed pixel-art aesthetic, hard pixel edges, dark one-pixel outlines, limited stepped shading, no gradients, no fine painted details. Full silhouette fits the entire square with about 1–2 logical pixels of clear padding. Original character design: do not reuse or resemble Crawl's angel, titan, tengu, or existing named monsters. No halo, no sword, no spear, no shield, no armor, no labels, no text, no UI, no ground shadow, no scenery. Background must be genuinely transparent, not a drawn checkerboard. Single character only, no sheet and no variants.
