Dungeon Crawl Chili v1.0-a0-... (through 1.0-a0-1650 )
------------------------------------------------------

Highlights
----------
* Scrolls and potions are identified when the game begins.
* Artefact generation rates have been doubled.
* Malevolent Forces have been removed.
* Monster descriptions use brown "DC Chili change" notes to explain fork-
  specific behaviour, including unique spawn locations and special drops.
* Most uniques have a 5% chance for one of their normal weapons, armour pieces,
  rings, or shields to be upgraded to an artefact.
* Many uniques have a 50% chance to drop a unique item when killed. The item
  depends on the monster and may be unavailable if it has already been found.
* The WebTiles inventory bar has been redesigned and is vertical by default.
* AI Coaching Help and the Post-Mortem Analysis are available only on the DCF 
  WebTiles server.
* A Death Replay functionality has been added to both offline Tiles and online
  WebTiles. The Webtiles functionality is only active on DCF at the moment.
* 16 new Uniques including a new Unique for each of the 4 Hell branches.
* Nine retired uniques return exclusively in the Necropolis: Michael, Duane,
  Francis, Norbert, Terpsichore, Jozef, Pan, Lamia and Maud. One awake unique
  roams each Necropolis, chosen to suit the entrance's difficulty level.
* A new background: Archaeologist, imported from BCrawl. They start with
  exploration equipment and decipher a dusty tome into acquirement at XL 5.
* A new background: Priest, imported from BCadren Crawl. They start in a chapel 
  with an altar to each god.
* 1 new species imported from BCrawl: Fairies are small flying spellcasters with
  cheaper spells, shimmering defensive scales and no body armour.
* 2 new species adapted from BcadrenCrawl:
  * Lignifites, a growing treefolk, grow larger, tougher and slower, and can root 
    themselves from XL 12.
  * Silent Spcepters, have a halo of silence around them and cannot read scrolls.

Branches and dungeon floors
---------------------------
* Dungeon and branch lengths were reduced by one floor, except for Orc, Elf,
  and Crypt. Unique spawn rates were increased to help replace the lost XP.
* XP gained from kills was also increased by 10%.
* A floor can have at most four uniques.
* Out-of-depth monster generation in the Dungeon was reduced from 7% to 3%.
* D:1 is a mostly forested, semi-open floor with a guaranteed faded altar and
  a basic shop for the 270 gold players start with.
* D:2 is mostly forested and semi-open, with dead trees, at least two altars,
  and Jessica almost guaranteed to appear.
* D:3 has corrupted trees and eight possible layouts, including a castle.
  Medusa is guaranteed to appear there.
* D:4 uses BcadrenCrawl's sewer layout, with Oskar almost guaranteed. The
  standard DCSS Sewer branch has been removed.
* D:5 is a catacomb with Menkaure almost guaranteed. The standard DCSS Ossuary
  has been removed.
* D:6 has a hot-and-cold theme with four central vaults. 
* D:7 has a central  abandoned nature reserve with three possible central vaults.

Early Unique encounters and rewards
-----------------------------
* Jessica is almost guaranteed on D:2 and has a 50% chance to drop a small
  spellbook. A dropped book has a 50% chance to contain Blink.
* Pooka can appear on D:2 and has a 50% chance to drop a Protean talisman.
* Medusa is guaranteed on D:3. She may carry the Talisman of Medusa; when she
  dies, nearby statues become adders and a water moccasin emerges from a
  fountain.
* Oskar is a D:4 unique who throws garbage bags and has a 50% chance to drop a
  potion of cancellation.

Other additions and adjustments
-------------------------------
* From BcadrenCrawl: the Leiomanos weapon in Shoals, potions of beneficial 
  mutation and stat gain, and scrolls of bless item. Wooden weapons can no
  longer receive the Flaming brand.
* The XP value is shown in monster descriptions. 
* Black Mark was reworked:
  tier 1 keeps its 20% Hex enhancer, tier 2's proc chance rises from 20% to
  50%, and tier 3 replaces the silence aura with an engulf attack and silent
  casting. The silence aura mutation became a three-tier halo that does not
  silence its owner.
* Formicids and tengu can wear hats; felids can wear hats and boots; and
  octopodes can wear scarves and hats. 
* Monsters no longer blink randomly when they turn invisible.
* Corrosive Blob was added as a level 7 spell, and staves of summoning that
  heal allies were reintroduced.
