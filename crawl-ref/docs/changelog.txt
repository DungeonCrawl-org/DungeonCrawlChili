Dungeon Crawl Chili v1.0-a0-... (through 1.0-a0-1650 )
--------------------------------------------------

Highlights
----------
* A new Unique for each of the 4 Hell branches.
* AI Coaching Help and Post-Mortem Analysis are now WebTiles-only features.
  Local Tiles no longer provides their menus, shortcuts or AI account access.
* Nine retired uniques return exclusively in the Necropolis: Michael, Duane,
  Francis, Norbert, Terpsichore, Jozef, Pan, Lamia and Maud. One awake unique
  roams each Necropolis, chosen to suit the entrance's difficulty.
* A new background: Archaeologist, imported from BCrawl. They start with
  exploration equipment and decipher a dusty tome into acquirement at XL 5.
* A new species: Fairies, imported from BCrawl. Small flying spellcasters with
  cheaper spells, shimmering defensive scales and no body armour.
* A new species: Lignifites, growing treefolk adapted from BcadrenCrawl.
  They grow larger, tougher and slower, and can root themselves from XL 12.
